NgRx & Component Store — Topic Roadmap
Topic 51 — NgRx Core Concepts (Store, Actions, Reducers, Selectors)
The foundational mental model: unidirectional data flow, immutable state, and why the four pieces exist as separate concepts rather than one object.
Topic 52 — NgRx Effects
Side effects outside the reducer: HTTP calls, navigation, analytics, and the operators that make Effects safe (concatMap vs switchMap vs exhaustMap in the Effects context).
Topic 53 — NgRx Selectors In Depth
Memoised projector functions, selector composition, createSelectorFactory, and why selectors are the single most performance-critical piece of an NgRx app.
Topic 54 — NgRx Entity
EntityState, EntityAdapter, and CRUD operations without boilerplate — the pattern that replaces manual array manipulation in reducers.
Topic 55 — NgRx Component Store
Local ephemeral state for a single component tree: when to use Component Store instead of the global Store, the updater/effect/select API, and the lifecycle tie-in.
Topic 56 — NgRx SignalStore (@ngrx/signals)
The Signal-native alternative to Component Store: signalStore, withState, withComputed, withMethods — and when to choose SignalStore over Component Store or global Store.
Topic 57 — NgRx Router Store
Connecting the Angular Router to the NgRx Store: serialising route params and query params as state, and selecting navigation data without injecting ActivatedRoute everywhere.
Topic 58 — NgRx Testing (Store, Effects, Selectors, Component Store)
MockStore, provideMockStore, overriding selectors, testing Effects with hot/cold observables, and testing Component Store in isolation.
Topic 59 — NgRx Best Practices & Architecture Patterns
Feature state organisation, action hygiene (one action per event), avoiding selector waterfalls, the facade pattern, and the rules that separate maintainable NgRx from spaghetti NgRx.
