06 The 5 Rules That Make This Work
  • Never move the gym

    It is your physical and mental recovery. Every developer who cuts recovery to study more ends up burning out and stopping entirely. The gym stays — always.

  • Commute time is not wasted time

    2 hours of daily commute on office days = 10 hours per week of learning time most developers throw away on social media. Protect it with the same seriousness as a scheduled meeting.

  • The WFH morning block is sacred

    Do not let it drift to 10am. Do not let Slack pull you in early. 8:00–9:30am is learning time. Your employer gets you from 10am. Those 90 minutes are the highest-value learning time in your entire week.

  • Friday is never a new topic day

    Friday is always review and consolidation. This is the rule that prevents the "I learned 10 things this week and retained none of them" problem. Review → fix unclear → only then advance.

  • A missed day has one rule: continue

    Acknowledge it, log it, start the next session as if nothing happened. No catching up. No doubling sessions. No guilt spiral. The developer who does 30 minutes every day for 6 months is not the same developer who started.
